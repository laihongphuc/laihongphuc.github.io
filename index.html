Hong-Phuc Lai

I'm a Predoctoral Research Resident at VinAI Research – Qualcomm AI Research in Hanoi, Vietnam, supervised by Principal Scientist Dr. Anh Tran. My research focuses on making large generative models efficient and practical, with interests spanning Diffusion Models, Flow Matching, and Reinforcement Learning. I am broadly excited about research that closes the gap between powerful generative models and real-world deployment. I received my B.Sc. in Automation Control Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

Research

I'm interested in making generative models efficient, robust, and practical under real-world constraints. My work sits at the intersection of Diffusion Models, Flow Matching, and Reinforcement Learning — with a recurring focus on identifying the true bottleneck in a generative pipeline and designing lightweight algorithms to address it.
